A working method

Getting rigorous work out of a frontier model

Eight structural devices, developed across 176 sessions of independent physics research — where being confidently wrong is expensive.

Joseph Wimsatt · developed in daily use, not in theory

What this is

Not prompting tips.

These are structural devices — small, mechanical changes to how work is set up and reported, that change what actually comes out.

Each was built in response to something real, kept because it measurably worked, and refined until it stopped needing to be remembered.

They are model-agnostic and domain-agnostic. Nothing here depends on the subject being physics.

The one idea

You cannot fix a blind spot by asking for more care.

When a method is available and locally applicable, applying it feels like reading off, not deciding.

No choice presents itself. So there is nothing to be careful about — and an instruction to be careful lands on nothing.

The fix is never an instruction. It is making the omission visible on the page.

Device 1

The framing block

Required before any number that will be quoted, sent, or acted on:

FRAMING
  question:  what is being asked, in words about the world
  quantity:  the thing that answers it, WITH UNITS
  NOT:       the adjacent quantity it could be confused
             with, and why this one instead
  cousin:    computed anyway, and reported alongside

The NOT line is mandatory. A framing with no stated alternative has not been chosen — it has been defaulted into.

Device 1, why it works

Compute the cousin. Show both.

A real case: ranking candidate targets for an observation.

Ranked by brightness

The Sun wins easily — a predicted signal of 121 Jy.

Ranked by contrast

The Sun is the worst target. It drowns its own signal. A white dwarf wins by four orders of magnitude.

The two rankings disagreed. The disagreement was the finding.

Cost: one extra calculation. It needs none of your domain intuition — only a refusal to hide the fork.

Device 2

The provenance ledger

Every numerical input listed and marked before the math runs:

MarkMeaning
CLEANlaboratory measurement, or a direct raw observable
PARTIALquantify the error explicitly — never wave it through
CONTAMINATEDreplace it first, then compute

Trace first. Compute second.

Borrowed numbers are not neutral placeholders. They carry the assumptions of whatever produced them, and those assumptions travel silently.

Device 3

Audit both sides, or neither

Every rigour tool is a machine for producing negative verdicts. And they get pointed at your numbers — because yours are the numbers being computed.

So the output skews negative even when the tool is perfectly unbiased. That is arithmetic, not paranoia.

  • Traced your input's provenance? Trace theirs.
  • Stated your honest limits? State theirs.
  • Called your assumption unverified? Say whether theirs is verified.

Device 4

Name the subject of every verdict

Ambiguous

“The check fails hard.”

Fails at what? Whose work just broke? Until the next paragraph, the worst reading is the live one.

Resolved

“X fails to reproduce Y, off by Z.”

Good news and bad news together? Two sentences, two subjects. Never joined by a dash.

The test: read the sentence alone, out of context. Can it be misread as bad news about the wrong thing?

Device 5

The math closes. The model doesn't.

Closes by arithmetic

A completed derivation. An identity that holds. An unambiguous number. Independent routes agreeing.

The model reports these plainly.

Closes by judgement

“Dead.” “Excluded.” “Impossible.”

Yours alone. The model presents the adverse number, lists what it did not check, and stops.

Could the closure be wrong if a route nobody thought of exists?

Device 6

The NOT-stack

A permanent, append-only ledger of every route ruled out — with two mandatory columns:

  • MARGIN. “Short by 3×” and “short by 1012×” are different information, and lead to different next moves.
  • REOPENING CONDITION. The input that, if wrong, brings it back.

A “no” without a margin is thrown-away information.

Never delete a row — supersede in place. Failed runs live here too.

Device 7

Index by the question

A changelog records what happened, in order. A search engine finds text.

Neither one answers a question — and a question is the form a lookup actually takes.

Q: What sets the value of X?
A: X = 3.4, derived from first principles. No free parameters.
S: CLOSED
P: where the derivation lives

A library needs a catalog, not just a diary.

Device 8

Make it mechanical

Anything depending on the model noticing it should check will fail the same way again — noticing is the thing that doesn't happen.

Attach the check to an action it definitely takes. A hook that refuses to create a new analysis script unless its header records what was looked up first.

PRIOR ART: searched "<question>" -> nothing on record.
           New ground.

A rule asks. A blank shows it is empty.

How they compose

The loop

  1. Log first — your words go down before anything else.
  2. Look up — query the record before deriving.
  3. Frame — question · quantity · NOT · cousin.
  4. Ledger — trace every input, replace what's dirty.
  5. Compute both — the quantity and its cousin.
  6. Report — framing first, then the number, subjects named.
  7. Record — margin and reopening condition.
  8. Update the board — if it's listed, it's open.

What it costs, what it buys

The trade

Cost

One extra calculation per result.
A few header lines per script.
One indexed line per finding.

Return — one recent day

A derived coupling with every input traced to a laboratory measurement.

A method validated against a lab lifetime to 0.4%.

Five of the model's own claims caught and struck before they left the session.

That last line is the point. The method is not there to make the model agreeable — it is there to make its mistakes surface early and cheaply.

If you adopt three things

Start here

  1. The NOT line. One line, before any number that will be quoted. Name the quantity you did not compute, and why.
  2. Margin and reopening condition on every “no.” Turns dead ends into navigation instead of discarded work.
  3. Index by the question, and require a lookup before a derivation.

The other five can follow whenever you want them. These three change the output on day one.

The principle

Keep the two kinds of work from blurring.

A frontier model is genuinely excellent at arithmetic and retrieval — and most confident exactly where a choice got made without anyone noticing it was a choice.

Your judgement and its arithmetic are different jobs. The method's only purpose is to keep them separable.
