A working method

Diagnosing a production line with an AI assistant

Eight devices that keep a fast answer from being a confident wrong one — and that stop the same fault being re-diagnosed every year.

Method: Joseph Wimsatt. Developed in daily research use, adapted here to the plant floor.

What this actually targets

Not bad arithmetic.

The expensive failure is correct work on the wrong quantity, from an unexamined input, reported without a subject.

A model will do the arithmetic perfectly and answer a question nobody asked. On a line, that costs a shift.

Everything here is structural. None of it requires the assistant to be smarter, and none of it slows down a stoppage — see the split on slide 14.

The one idea

You cannot fix a blind spot by asking for more care.

When a metric is available and looks applicable, using it feels like reading it off, not choosing it.

No choice presents itself — so “be careful” lands on nothing.

The fix is making the omission visible on the page.

Device 1

The framing block

“Downtime is up 12%.” Up in minutes lost, or in number of stoppages?

Minutes up, stops flat

One long failure. A breakdown. Maintenance owns it.

Stops up, minutes flat

Chronic micro-stops. A feed, jam or sensor issue. Different team, different fix.

Same data. Opposite diagnoses.

The NOT line forces the choice into the open before anyone acts on it.

Device 1, in use

Compute the cousin. Show both.

FRAMING
  question: why did Station 4's reject rate rise this week
  quantity: rejects per 1,000 fastenings, by shift
  NOT:      rejects per HOUR -- throughput fell 20% when the
            line slowed, so per-hour would have hidden it
  cousin:   computed anyway. Per-hour is FLAT.
            Per-unit is up 4x.

The two disagreed. That disagreement was the finding.

One extra query. It needs no process expertise — only a refusal to hide the fork.

Device 2

The provenance ledger

MarkOn a line
CLEANraw PLC tag, timestamped, unaggregated. A physical count.
PARTIALany average or resample. A 1-minute mean of a 10 Hz signal hides the spike that caused the fault.
CONTAMINATEDoperator-entered downtime reason codes. Spec-sheet “expected” cycle time. A golden-batch baseline taken under different conditions.

Reason codes are treated as data. They are a person under time pressure picking the first item in a dropdown. Mark them, or the whole diagnosis inherits them.

Device 3

Audit both sides, or neither

  • Before you conclude Line 3 has a problem, run the identical query against Line 2. If the anomaly is there too, it is your sensor, your query, or your data pipeline.
  • Before you blame the new supplier, audit the incumbent's lots the same way. Very often the incumbent looks just as bad and nobody ever looked.

Any tool that finds problems will find them wherever you point it.

So point it at the baseline too — in the same pass, to the same depth.

Device 4

Name the subject of every verdict

“The check failed” on a line can mean four different things — and they go to four different people.

  • The part failed.
  • The test failed.
  • The test equipment failed.
  • The data feed failed.

“Station 4's torque check rejected 8% against a 2% baseline, over three shifts.”

One sentence. One owner. An ambiguous alert burns a shift.

Device 5

The math closes. The assistant doesn't.

It may close

“The tag was out of range for 90 consecutive samples.”

“These two timestamps differ by 4.2 s.”

Arithmetic. Checkable. Not a judgement.

It may not close

“The bearing is failing.”

“It is safe to run to Friday.”

“Root cause is the servo drive.”

Could it be wrong if a cause nobody thought of exists?

Then it is a judgement, and it belongs to the engineer who signs for it.

Device 6

The NOT-stack

Per asset. Every route ruled out, kept permanently, with a margin and a reopening condition:

Ruled outMarginReopens if
air pressure6.2 bar vs 5.5 needed — 13% headroomcompressor duty > 80%
servo tuningdrive replaced, fault back in 4 h—
ambient temperatureoccurs at 18°C and 31°C alikehumidity was never measured

That last row is the whole argument. “Ruled out temperature” would have thrown away the fact that humidity was never checked. The next person starts at row four, not row one.

Device 7

Index by the question

A maintenance log is a diary. “11 March: replaced sensor, ran fine.” Nobody can find that again.

Q: Why does Station 7 fault on the first cycle
   after a cold start?
A: Condensate in the air line. Drains on warm-up.
S: CLOSED -- verified across 3 winters
P: work order 4412, trace attached

Tribal knowledge stops walking out the door.

This is the difference between a system you can archive into and one you can actually ask.

Device 8

Make it mechanical

Anything relying on someone remembering to check will fail the same way again — at 3am, on overtime, with the line down.

So attach the check to a step that always happens. A diagnostic report that cannot be filed until two fields are non-empty:

WHAT I CHECKED FIRST: searched the asset's NOT-stack
                      -> nothing on record. New fault.
INPUTS AND MARKS:     torque trace CLEAN,
                      reason codes CONTAMINATED (unused)

A rule asks. A blank shows it is empty.

Worked end to end

One fault, all eight

FRAMING   rejects per 1,000 units, NOT per hour.
          Cousin computed: per-hour flat, per-unit 4x.

LEDGER    torque trace 1 kHz          CLEAN
          reject flag from tester     CLEAN
          downtime reason codes       CONTAMINATED - unused
          ambient temp, 15-min mean   PARTIAL - cycle is 8 s,
                                      the average cannot resolve it

BOTH SIDES  same query run on Station 5: baseline normal.
            So it is Station 4, not the pipeline.

VERDICT   "Station 4 rejects rose 4x per unit over 3 shifts."
          NOT "the torque gun is failing" - that is a judgement.

RECORDED  NOT-stack row added, with margin and reopening
          condition. Indexed by the question.

The honest limit

You cannot run the loop with the line down.

Live — seconds

Name the subject.

State the NOT line.

That is all. Both are one sentence each.

Post-mortem — after restart

The full provenance ledger.

The NOT-stack row, with margin and reopening condition.

The question-index entry.

Any method that claims otherwise gets discarded on first contact with a real stoppage — and deserves to be.

Why it pays back faster here

Research asks once. A plant asks forever.

In research, most questions are asked a single time. On a line, the same intermittent fault is re-diagnosed by different people, across shifts, sites and years.

Each one re-checks the same five things — because the last person's “ruled that out” was recorded without a margin, without a reopening condition, or not recorded at all.

The two devices that compound with repetition are worth more on a line than in a laboratory.

The NOT-stack and the question index are a direct fix for knowledge that leaves when someone retires.

The principle

Keep the two kinds of work from blurring.

An AI assistant is genuinely excellent at arithmetic, correlation and recall — and most confident exactly where a choice got made without anyone noticing it was a choice.

Your engineering judgement and its arithmetic are different jobs. The method's only purpose is to keep them separable.

Everything else on these slides is detail.
